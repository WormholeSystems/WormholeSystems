# Creating a map

From the **Maps** page, click **New map** in the top-right (or **Create your first map** if you don't have any yet).

Fill in:

- **Name**: whatever you want to call the chain (for example "Home", or your corp's name). Required, up to 255 characters.
- **Description**: optional, what the chain is for.

Click **Create** and you're taken straight to the new, empty map.

Moving a map over from the old app? Use **Import** next to **New map** instead; see [Moving from the legacy app](/documentation/getting-started/moving-from-legacy).

## What you get

- **You're the owner.** As the creator you have full control — you can edit everything, manage who else has access, and you're the only one who can delete the map. See [Roles](/documentation/access-and-permissions/roles).
- **A few hubs are pre-loaded.** The major trade hubs — Jita, Amarr, Dodixie, Rens, and Hek — are added to your [watch list](/documentation/navigation/watch-list) and pinned, so route planning works the moment you start mapping.

From here you'll usually be greeted by the [introduction](/documentation/getting-started/the-introduction), then you can start [adding systems](/documentation/getting-started/adding-systems-and-connections) or simply fly the chain and let it [map itself](/documentation/getting-started/auto-mapping).
